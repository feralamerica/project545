Beyond 545: Israel's Other Registered Campaigns

Project 545 is one budget: 545 million shekels, two ministries, nine registered agents. It is not the whole picture. The engagements below are documented from their own FARA filings and are kept deliberately separate from the Project 545 count: different money, different principals, different purposes. Every engagement here is lawful and registered; no wrongdoing is asserted by anyone named. American advocacy organizations are not foreign agents and appear nowhere on this page. The full live list of every registrant DOJ tags with an Israel-connected principal is in Registrants.

Why this page exists: the cleanliness of the disclosure tracks the audience. Defense committees and bond desks get contracts with visible fees; the general public gets mesh websites and texting personas. Same principal country, different manners for different rooms.

Methodology

Sources and tiers

Tier A: sworn federal filings in DOJ's FARA database, read in full and linked directly throughout this site. Tier B: published reporting, credited by name. Nothing here rests on anonymous sourcing.

The daily sync

The Filings and Registrants pages are rebuilt every day from the Department of Justice's public FARA bulk data files (registrants, foreign principals, registrant documents, short forms). The script filters to every registrant that has had an Israel-connected foreign principal active since 2023, keeps the complete docket for the Project 545 legs, and keeps the Israel-tagged filings for everyone else. Nothing in that data is edited; dates, names and document URLs are DOJ's own, typos included. The sync code is public in the site's repository.

Our calculations, labeled as ours

Any figure not printed in a filing is our computation from that filing's own itemized appendix, marked with an asterisk. Principal example: the Show Faith by Works May 2026 supplemental swears total disbursements of $3,333,191.86; our parse of all 742 appendix lines found every transaction listed an even number of times, zero exceptions, for deduplicated actual spending of exactly $1,666,595.93, precisely half the sworn figure. A clerical origin for the duplication is plausible and is noted wherever the finding appears. The company did not respond to detailed questions by the stated deadline. Its October 2026 supplemental is not doubled.

Draft material

Entries marked DRAFT were read from filings made after August 7, 2026 and summarized from the PDFs (several of which are scanned and were machine-read). They are published so the record is current, and they carry the tag until each figure has been re-read against the document by hand and comment requests have gone out. Treat a DRAFT figure as a pointer to the document, not as a finding.

What this tracker does not claim

No assertion of candidate advocacy, race targeting, electoral intent, coordination with any US campaign, or unlawful conduct by any person, except where a filing itself discloses a fact. Sequence is not causation; open questions are labeled as questions; lawful conduct (disclosed personal political contributions, commercial registered agents, timely registrations) is identified as lawful.

Comment and corrections

Every registrant receives detailed questions with a stated deadline before coverage naming them publishes; responses are added in full, and non-responses are dated. Documented errors are corrected loudly, not quietly, and logged on the Updates page. Corrections with documentation: localtip@proton.me.
